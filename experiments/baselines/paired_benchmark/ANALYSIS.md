# Task 8 analysis plan

This plan is fixed before reading formal outcomes. The formal protocol has 30 paired seeds within each of three dimensions and two contamination conditions: 180 cases and 3,600 method records. Smoke results are implementation checks and are rejected by formal analysis.

- The primary endpoint is absolute known synthetic target error at T=0. Origin is an exact truth reference for this task and is not evidence of useful general inversion. There is no pooled evaluator in this experiment. Each case separately records the unconditional analytic origin reference of zero, including cases with no qualified coalition; failed method records remain failed and are not imputed to zero.
- The unit is seed within each dimension/contamination condition. All methods share the case's generated observations and initial model state. Paired intervals use the same resampled seeds. There are no zero-error imputations, replacement seeds, or silent exclusions; counts of planned, valid, failed and valid pairs are reported.
- Each method/budget/condition gets mean, seed SD, and descriptive ordinary 95% bootstrap intervals. Bootstrap uses 200,000 replicates and rng 20261123. A hard-budget stop with a finite box-feasible previously evaluated decision is a valid delivery; optimizer success and stop reason remain separate.
- The primary panel budget is 400. The five contrasts are best_of minus each of best_anchor, anchor_mean, nelder_mead at 400, fedavg_all, and fedavg_selected. Six conditions times five contrasts form one Bonferroni family of 30 intervals. The other budgets, engine ablations, individual-method means and resource statistics are descriptive. The primary family is not a guarantee across all endpoints or analyses.
- Report prediction requests and input samples, payload bytes and training communication rounds, qualification calls, local input-gradient work and training samples separately. Per-arm payload totals do not establish matched end-to-end costs, wall-clock latency, encryption overhead, privacy, or fairness.
- FedAvg all-participant and selected-coalition controls use different admission and training communication. Selected-coalition FedAvg also incurs the local-training and qualification costs required to choose that coalition.
- Validate coverage, unique keys, finite errors and decisions, box feasibility, per-row hard budgets, the exact origin reference, protocol/source hashes and each case/completion/data/model/trace hash. Missing or failed deliveries stay visible. The analysis is a new-seed synthetic benchmark after historical solver development, not an externally validated or universally superior method.
- Plot truth error by panel budget in six dimension/contamination facets for best_of, custom, coefficient, Nelder–Mead and direct SLSQP. FedAvg controls and best-anchor means appear as horizontal references; origin is indicated separately by its exact zero error. Curves compare the search schedules, not full-system resource equality.
